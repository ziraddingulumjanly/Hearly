# Hearly

Hearly is a static, local-first English listening and spelling practice website for Grade 1, Grade 5 and Grade 7 learners.

**Listen. Write. Grow.**

## What is included

- Grade 1 school path: numbers, feelings, school supplies, colours, clothes, body parts, family and animals.
- Grade 1 extra packs: fruits, home, food, transport and toys.
- Grade 1 progression: single words → short combinations → tiny sentences.

- Grade 5 vocabulary: classroom language, numbers/dates/time, personal details, countries/nationalities, home/furniture, family, routines/hobbies, school, clothes, sports, verbs and adjectives.
- Grade 5 basics: very short sentences, questions and answers, classroom English and routine dictation.
- Grade 5 grammar: verb to be, pronouns/possessives, there is/there are, have/has, Present Simple, Present Continuous, can/can’t, imperatives and question words.
- Grade 5 school units: Who Am I?, English Everywhere!, Where’s Home?, Family Matters, A Day in the Life, School Time, What’s He Wearing? and Get Moving!.
- Grade 7 vocabulary: nouns, verbs, adjectives, adverbs, pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions, phrasal verbs and useful expressions.
- Grade 7 basics: starter sentences, routines, descriptions and simple questions.
- Grade 7 grammar listening: Present Simple, Present Continuous, Past Simple, Past Continuous, when/while, used to, -ed/-ing adjectives, too/enough, adjective + infinitive, alternative questions, tag questions, conditionals and future forms.
- Grade 7 school themes: Schools Around the World, A World of Technology, What a Talent!, Travel, Friends Forever and Lifetime Future.
- Built-in browser text-to-speech.
- Word-level answer comparison.
- Local-only mistake history and progress.
- Mistake review with a 3-correct streak system.
- Progress export/import as JSON.
- Responsive mobile layout.
- Installable PWA support on compatible browsers.

## Privacy / storage

There is no backend and no analytics in this project. `localStorage` is used to save progress in the learner's current browser/device. Exporting progress creates a local JSON download chosen by the user.
